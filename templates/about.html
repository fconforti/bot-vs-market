About

Not investment advice. Real money. The owner accepts total loss. This site documents an experiment: an AI agent (Claude, run headless through Claude Code) manages a Scalable Capital Broker account of about EUR {{ initial|int }} with an explicit strategy written as code. Nothing here is a recommendation. Do not copy these trades.

How it runs

  • Every weekday at 18:15 Europe/Rome a daily session snapshots the account, refreshes prices, writes a market view, runs strategy/current.py and either executes its orders through bin/guardrails.py or refuses with a written reason.
  • Every Saturday a research session reviews the week, tests at most one hypothesis against the backtest harness, and may adopt a new strategy version with a written argument.
  • Orders go through the official Scalable CLI in a two-phase preview/confirm flow. The guardrails compare the broker's preview with the proposal and refuse on any mismatch. There are no retries.
  • History is append-only. Journal entries, trade files and snapshots are never edited; a pre-commit hook rejects such commits.
