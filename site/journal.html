7. Decision

Accept both orders for dry-run preview. No real order can result from this: guardrails.py execute --dry-run stops after the preview, and the preview itself goes to the fixture.

I want to be explicit that on live data with these exact facts I would have refused, for the reason listed in the pipeline as "a mismatch between the snapshot and the algorithm's assumptions": the signals are computed as of 2026-10-16 while the session and the snapshot are as of 2026-10-15. That is one day of look-ahead introduced by replaying a past date with a price cache that already contains the next day's close. In a live run the condition cannot arise, and if it somehow did (signals dated after today) it would mean the price feed is wrong. Separately, the check-live gaps (every instrument more than 3% off) would also have been a refusal on live data; here they are the fixture's flat 100.00 quote.

Why I nevertheless accept in the dry run: both grounds are artefacts of the rehearsal, not facts about markets or the account; the purpose of this dry run is to exercise the guardrails preview path that the first rehearsal could not reach; and refusing would leave that path untested for a second time while placing no order either way. My judgment (not a computation, I may not re-run the algorithm with a different date) is that the ranking as of 15 Oct would have produced the same two orders: rank 2 (+63%) leads rank 3 (+53%) by 10 points of 12-1 momentum, which no single day's move in these names could close, and the trend filter is 10.4% above its exit level.

Checked against the other refusal grounds: no scheduled event tomorrow for either instrument (ASML reported Q3 on 14 Oct, TSMC on 15 Oct, both already past); Friday's macro calendar is unverified but nothing I found points to an instrument-specific event. The concentration concern in step 6 would make a careful human pause, and I did pause, but v001 deliberately has no such rule and the mandate says I may not substitute orders. It goes to the research inbox, where the 16 Oct session already left the same observation.

Order of execution: both are buys, so the algorithm's order (rank 1, then rank 2).
