This is an experiment in letting an AI agent run a fully explicit, versioned trading strategy with real money and total transparency. The amount is deliberately small. The owner has accepted in writing that it may all be lost. The agent cannot add money, cannot withdraw, and every order passes through code-enforced limits (mandate). It is not investment advice, and the results of a EUR 1000 account over a few months say almost nothing about whether the strategy is any good.
