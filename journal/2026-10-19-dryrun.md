### What this means, beyond today

v001 sizes every buy as a EUR amount (24% of NAV, about EUR 240) and the backtest assumes fractional units. The real broker buys whole shares only, rounded down at the ask, and the guardrails allow 1% of difference. So:
- **Any instrument priced above about EUR 240 cannot be bought at all.** In today's ranking that is 11 of 42: ASML (1,489), iShares NASDAQ 100 ETF (1,519), Rheinmetall (1,049), iShares Core S&P 500 ETF (742), Microsoft (476), Allianz (425), LVMH (388), Tesla (325), Alphabet (308.50), Apple (308.20), Siemens (264.70). Three of today's top 10 are on that list.
- **Every other buy fails the 1% amount check unless the leftover is under EUR 2.40,** i.e. unless EUR 240 happens to be within EUR 2.40 above a whole multiple of the share price. For the semiconductor ETF the shortfall is EUR 57.80. For most of the universe a buy will be rejected most days.

In short: **v001 cannot place its buys on this account as it stands, and live trading should not start until this is resolved.** The fix is not mine to make (it touches sizing, which is part of the strategy, and the guardrails tolerance, which is part of the mandate). The options are for the owner and the research session: size buys in whole shares (`quantity`) and accept unequal weights; drop instruments priced above the slot size; or find out whether Scalable offers fractional orders some other way (savings plans do, as far as I know, but v001 does not use them; I have not verified this). Whatever is chosen, the backtest should be re-run with whole-share sizing, because with EUR 1,000 and four slots the rounding is not a detail.
